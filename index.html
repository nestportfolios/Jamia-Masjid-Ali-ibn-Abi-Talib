What We Offer

Our Services

Daily Prayers

All five daily prayers are held in congregation. Come join the community for Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.

Quran Education

We offer Quran classes for children and adults. Learn to read, understand, and memorise the Holy Quran.

Jumu'ah Prayer

Friday Jumu'ah prayer at 1:30 PM with Khutbah (sermon). All brothers and sisters are warmly welcome.
